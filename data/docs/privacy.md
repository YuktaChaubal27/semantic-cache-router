# Privacy and your data

## Exporting your data
Export all your data under Settings > Privacy > Export. You receive an email with a ZIP file download link, usually within 24 hours.

## Data retention
When account deletion completes after the grace period, your data is permanently erased within 30 days. Invoices are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it.

## Selling data
We never sell your personal data to third parties.

## Who can see your data
In a Team workspace, admins can see all workspace content. Members can see content in the projects they are added to. Our staff access your data only when you ask support for help.
