# Refund policy

## European Union
Customers in the EU may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase without giving a reason, as required by consumer law.

## United States
Customers in the US may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds after 30 days are issued as account credit.

## Annual plans
Annual plans can be refunded for unused full months during the first 60 days. After 60 days, annual plans are not refundable but can be set not to renew.

## How to request a refund
Request a refund from Billing > Refunds, or contact support with your invoice number.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. The delay comes from banks and card networks, not from us.
