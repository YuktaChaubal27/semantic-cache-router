# Plans and subscriptions

## Plans
We offer three plans: Free, Pro and Team. Pro adds unlimited projects. Team adds shared workspaces and admin controls for up to 50 members. Pro and Team can be billed monthly or annually.

## Upgrading
Upgrades take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing cycle.

## Downgrading
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, so you keep your current features until then.

## Cancelling
Cancel anytime under Billing > Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and your data is kept. You can reactivate a cancelled subscription at any time.

## Pausing
You can pause a subscription for up to 3 months. You are not charged while paused, and your data is kept.

## Auto-renewal
Subscriptions renew automatically. Turn off auto-renewal under Billing > Subscription; your plan then ends at the end of the current period.
