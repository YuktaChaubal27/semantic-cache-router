# Billing and payments

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Update your card under Billing > Payment methods.

## Currencies
Prices are available in USD, EUR and GBP. Your currency is set by your billing country and cannot be changed after the first payment.

## Invoices and receipts
Every payment generates an invoice, which doubles as your receipt. Download invoices as PDF files from Billing > Invoices.

## Duplicate charges
If you see two charges, one is usually a temporary authorization hold that your bank places to verify the card. Holds disappear on their own within 3 business days. If both charges remain after 3 business days, contact support and we will refund the duplicate.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry it 3 times over 7 days and email you each time. If all retries fail, your subscription is paused until you update your payment method. Your data is kept while the subscription is paused.
