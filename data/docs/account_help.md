# Account help

## Resetting your password
To reset your password, open Settings, choose Security, and click Reset password. A reset link is emailed to you and expires after 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication can be turned on under Settings > Security. We support authenticator apps and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported, because text messages can be intercepted through SIM-swapping. Authenticator apps are the easiest option; hardware security keys give the strongest protection against phishing.

## Changing your email or username
Change your email address under Settings > Profile. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you click it. Usernames can be changed once every 30 days from the same page.

## Locked accounts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes to protect it from password guessing. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password to unlock it immediately.

## Active sessions
See every device signed in to your account under Settings > Security > Active sessions. Click Sign out of all devices to end every session except the current one.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period. During the grace period you can sign in to cancel the deletion. Deleting your account also cancels any active subscription.
