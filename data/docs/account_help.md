# Account help

## Resetting your password
To reset your password, open Settings, choose Security, and click Reset password. A reset link is emailed to you and expires after 30 minutes.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication can be turned on under Settings > Security. We support authenticator apps and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period. During the grace period you can sign in to cancel the deletion.
